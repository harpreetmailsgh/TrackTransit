1. Overview & Commitment

Transit Scanner ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you use our mobile application and related services.

Current Version: Transit Scanner v1.0 stores your saved routes, preferences, and active ride state locally on your device. The app connects to external transit data services to download schedules, vehicle positions, service updates, and app data files. We do not sell user data, use advertising tracking, or run third-party analytics.

2. What Data We Collect

2.1 Location Data

Collection: When you grant permission, Transit Scanner accesses your device's location to display your position on the map and, when you start an active ride, provide get-off alert support.

Storage: Location data is used in real time. Transit Scanner may store active ride state locally so it can continue a ride alert session, but it does not send your location to Transit Scanner servers.

Permission Control: You can disable location access at any time through your device's system settings. Background location is used only for an active ride after you grant permission.

2.2 Route & Search History

Collection: When you save a route or perform a search, Transit Scanner stores this information locally on your device (e.g., which routes you save, search queries, trip details).

Storage: Stored only on your device's local storage. This data never leaves your phone unless you choose to export or share it.

Purpose: To provide quick access to your saved routes and personalize your experience.

2.3 Notification Preferences

Collection: If you enable notifications, Transit Scanner stores your notification preferences locally (enabled/disabled state for different alert types).

Storage: Notification preferences are stored locally. Transit Scanner uses local notifications for active-ride alerts and may request an Expo push notification token as part of notification setup; v1 does not send that token to a Transit Scanner backend.

2.4 Transit Data from GO Transit

Collection: Transit Scanner retrieves public real-time transit data, schedules, vehicle locations, service updates, and hosted SQLite app data files from external data providers.

Storage: Transit data is cached locally for performance and is not associated with your personal identity.

No Personal Association: This data is publicly available and is not personal data belonging to you.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Location Tracking: To show your position on the map and provide active-ride get-off alerts.
  • Route Personalization: To remember your saved routes and search preferences.
  • Notification Setup: To manage local alert preferences and active-ride reminders.
  • App Functionality: To display schedules, delays, and real-time vehicle information.

We DO NOT: Share, sell, or monetize any user data. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or marketing purposes.

4. Data Storage & Retention

Local Storage: All user preferences, saved routes, and search history are stored only on your device using standard mobile app storage mechanisms.

Retention Duration:

  • Saved Routes: Retained indefinitely until you manually delete them.
  • Search History: Retained indefinitely; can be manually cleared.
  • Location Data: Not persisted; used only during active app sessions.
  • Transit Data Cache: Stored locally for performance and refreshed as app data or live transit feeds are updated.

No Backup: In v1, data is not automatically backed up or synced to cloud services.

5. Sharing & Third Parties

Current Situation (v1): Transit Scanner does not sell user data or use advertising tracking. The app contacts external services needed to provide transit schedules, live transit information, hosted data files, and notification setup.

External APIs Used:

  • GO Transit / Metrolinx Data Services: Public transit schedules, service updates, vehicle positions, and related transit information.
  • GitHub Releases: Hosted SQLite transit data files used to reduce app startup memory usage.
  • Expo Notifications: Notification permission and token setup when notification features are enabled.

No Analytics or Tracking: We do not use third-party analytics services, crash reporting with personal data, or user tracking.

6. User Controls & Preferences

Location Permission: You can enable or disable location access at any time in your device settings. The app will function normally with location disabled; map features may be limited.

Delete Saved Routes: You can delete individual saved routes or clear all saved data directly in the app.

Local Search Data: Search and saved-route data remains on your device and can be removed through available in-app deletion controls or by deleting the app.

Notification Preferences: You can disable notifications at the app level or device level.

7. Security & Protection

Local Storage Security: Your device's operating system (iOS or Android) protects local app data through device-level encryption and access controls.

Network Transmission: Transit Scanner does not send your saved routes, searches, or location to Transit Scanner servers. The app does make HTTPS requests to external transit data and app data services required for functionality.

SSL/TLS for Public Data: All communication with GO Transit's data feed uses secure HTTPS connections.

No Cloud Storage: User data is not backed up to cloud services, eliminating the risk of unauthorized cloud storage access.

8. Children's Privacy

Transit Scanner is designed for users of all ages and is not restricted to adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 in a way that violates COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) or GDPR.

No Data Collection from Children: We do not collect names, email addresses, or other identifiable information from any user, regardless of age.

Parent Responsibility: Parents and guardians should be aware that the app accesses location data when permitted and should monitor permission settings on their child's device.

9. Future Updates & Backend Integration (v2+)

Backend Infrastructure: Transit Scanner is architected to support future cloud-based features in v2 and beyond, including:

  • Delay notification delivery via push notifications
  • User account synchronization across devices
  • Saved route backup and recovery
  • Anonymized usage analytics to improve the service

Privacy on Backend Transition: If and when Transit Scanner adds backend services, we will:

  • Update this Privacy Policy with full details before launch
  • Allow opt-in for cloud features; local-only mode will remain available
  • Maintain end-to-end encryption where feasible
  • Never sell or share user data with advertisers or third parties
  • Comply with all applicable privacy regulations (GDPR, PIPEDA, CCPA, COPPA, etc.)
