1. Overview & Commitment

TrackTransit ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you use our mobile application and related services.

Current Version: TrackTransit v1.0 operates in local-only mode. We do not collect, store, or transmit personal data to external servers. All data remains on your device unless you explicitly choose to enable features requiring external communication.

2. What Data We Collect

2.1 Location Data

Collection: When you grant permission, TrackTransit accesses your device's location to display your position on the map and calculate route information.

Storage: Location data is used in real-time only and is not stored by the app unless you explicitly interact with saved routes that reference your current position.

Permission Control: You can disable location access at any time through your device's system settings. TrackTransit will request permission before any location data is used.

2.2 Route & Search History

Collection: When you save a route or perform a search, TrackTransit stores this information locally on your device (e.g., which routes you save, search queries, trip details).

Storage: Stored only on your device's local storage. This data never leaves your phone unless you choose to export or share it.

Purpose: To provide quick access to your saved routes and personalize your experience.

2.3 Notification Preferences

Collection: If you enable notifications, TrackTransit stores your notification preferences locally (enabled/disabled state for different alert types).

Storage: Local device storage only. In v1, notifications are not sent to your device; this infrastructure is prepared for future v2 features.

2.4 Transit Data from GO Transit

Collection: TrackTransit retrieves public real-time transit data (schedules, vehicle locations, delays) from GO Transit's public data feeds.

Storage: Transit data is cached locally for performance and is not associated with your personal identity.

No Personal Association: This data is publicly available and is not personal data belonging to you.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Location Tracking: To show your position on the map and calculate route recommendations.
  • Route Personalization: To remember your saved routes and search preferences.
  • Notification Setup: To manage your notification preferences (prepared for v2 backend).
  • App Functionality: To display schedules, delays, and real-time vehicle information.

We DO NOT: Share, sell, or monetize any user data. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or marketing purposes.

4. Data Storage & Retention

Local Storage: All user preferences, saved routes, and search history are stored only on your device using standard mobile app storage mechanisms.

Retention Duration:

  • Saved Routes: Retained indefinitely until you manually delete them.
  • Search History: Retained indefinitely; can be manually cleared.
  • Location Data: Not persisted; used only during active app sessions.
  • Transit Data Cache: Refreshed hourly; older data is automatically purged.

No Backup: In v1, data is not automatically backed up or synced to cloud services.

5. Sharing & Third Parties

Current Situation (v1): TrackTransit does not share data with third parties. All data remains on your device.

External APIs Used:

  • GO Transit Data Feed: Public, anonymized transit data. Your usage of this data does not create a personal record associated with your identity.

No Analytics or Tracking: We do not use third-party analytics services, crash reporting with personal data, or user tracking.

6. User Controls & Preferences

Location Permission: You can enable or disable location access at any time in your device settings. The app will function normally with location disabled; map features may be limited.

Delete Saved Routes: You can delete individual saved routes or clear all saved data directly in the app.

Clear Search History: Available through the app settings.

Notification Preferences: You can disable notifications at the app level or device level.

7. Security & Protection

Local Storage Security: Your device's operating system (iOS or Android) protects local app data through device-level encryption and access controls.

No Network Transmission of Personal Data: TrackTransit does not send personal information over the network. Only public transit data requests are made to GO Transit's public API.

SSL/TLS for Public Data: All communication with GO Transit's data feed uses secure HTTPS connections.

No Cloud Storage: User data is not backed up to cloud services, eliminating the risk of unauthorized cloud storage access.

8. Children's Privacy

TrackTransit is designed for users of all ages and is not restricted to adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 in a way that violates COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) or GDPR.

No Data Collection from Children: We do not collect names, email addresses, or other identifiable information from any user, regardless of age.

Parent Responsibility: Parents and guardians should be aware that the app accesses location data when permitted and should monitor permission settings on their child's device.

9. Future Updates & Backend Integration (v2+)

Backend Infrastructure: TrackTransit is architected to support future cloud-based features in v2 and beyond, including:

  • Delay notification delivery via push notifications
  • User account synchronization across devices
  • Saved route backup and recovery
  • Anonymized usage analytics to improve the service

Privacy on Backend Transition: If and when TrackTransit adds backend services, we will:

  • Update this Privacy Policy with full details before launch
  • Allow opt-in for cloud features; local-only mode will remain available
  • Maintain end-to-end encryption where feasible
  • Never sell or share user data with advertisers or third parties
  • Comply with all applicable privacy regulations (GDPR, PIPEDA, CCPA, COPPA, etc.)
